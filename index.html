Homepage of Mohammad Mahmoody


Welcome to my homepage. I am a computer scientist with interests in foundations of cryptography as well as trustworthy AI from a principled point of view.

I am currently a faculty at Bilkent University. Previously, I got my PhD from Princeton in 2010 and joined Cornell as a postdoc. I joined the Computer Science Department of the University of Virginia in 2013 as an assistant professor, where I became an associate professor with tenure in 2019.
